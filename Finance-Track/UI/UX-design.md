## Design Requirements to be Added:

### 1. Color Palette & Aesthetics
- **Premium Touch Blue:** The primary theme color must be a "premium touch blue" (e.g., a deep, elegant navy or a sleek, modern royal blue). Specify the exact hex codes for primary, secondary, background, and accent colors to maintain consistency.
- **Minimalist Elegance:** The overall aesthetic should be highly minimalistic but strictly premium-looking. Avoid cluttering the screen with unnecessary elements.

### 2. Icons & Typography
- **Minimal Icons:** Do NOT add too many icons, as this can easily confuse the user. Use minimal, sleek, and universally recognizable icons for buttons, navigation, and actions.
- **Icon Style:** Avoid heavy or overly complex icon styles. Prefer thin, outlined, or simple, flat solid styles that look modern and clean.
- **Premium Typography:** Recommend clean, legible, modern sans-serif fonts that complement the premium minimalist look.

### 3. Layout & User Interface
- **Clean Interfaces:** Ensure ample whitespace (padding and margins) around elements to let the design breathe. The UI should guide the user naturally without overwhelming them.
- **Button Design:** Buttons should have subtle, elegant styling.

### 4. Animations & Transitions
- **Page Transitions:** Define smooth, proper transitions between screens and pages (e.g., subtle fade-ins, gentle shared-element transitions, or fluid slides). Avoid jarring or abrupt screen changes.
- **Micro-interactions:** Recommend subtle micro-animations that enhance the premium feel without being distracting (like a soft ripple on button press).
