Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

FinanceTrack collects the following information to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and encrypted password when you register.
  • Financial Data: Expenses, income, budgets, goals, account balances, and transaction details that you enter.
  • Usage Data: Login timestamps, IP addresses, and device information for security purposes.
  • Preferences: Currency, timezone, theme, and notification settings.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the FinanceTrack service.
  • Process your financial entries and generate reports.
  • Send you notifications about budgets, goals, and recurring transactions.
  • Ensure security and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your financial data is stored securely with the following protections:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt.
  • All API communications use JWT token authentication.
  • Database backups are encrypted and stored separately.
  • Access is restricted to authenticated users only.
  • Sensitive operations are logged in an audit trail.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties. Your data remains private and is only accessible to you.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings page.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your stored data via the Export feature.
  • Delete your account and all data permanently.
  • Modify or correct your information at any time.
  • Request a backup of your database.

7. Cookies

FinanceTrack uses JWT tokens stored in your browser for authentication. No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@financetrack.com
